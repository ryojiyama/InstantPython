１話　征服者

The grand audience room that could be mistaken for a royal palace. 玉座と言っても通用しそうな豪華な緋の椅子の前に、整然と列を作る十数人の人間。 They are divided into two types of clothing: light and dark.

人数が多いのは明るい色の服装。つい先日まで支配者の側に居た人間だ。 They all look restlessly with anxious eyes at their new master. Even if they had animosity toward the conquerors, they didn't have the courage to show it, considering they were soundly defeated by a number small enough to pass through the northern bypaths, even if their true master was away.

彼らの前、クルトハイト大公座に座っているのは一人の少女だ。明るい紫の長い髪と整った顔つき。 A black robe interwoven with patterns like gold thread. She places a finger of her right hand on her slender chin, while toying with something in her left hand, looking down at the five people who stepped forward from the line.

「１１番と、１７番はクレーヌに、２１番と２２番はベリトに。８番は……私が後で見ます。アリーに渡しなさい。クレーヌは進行中の５番と合わせて明後日までに報告」 The cold voice of Mateil, the Imperial Princess and the top commander of the Kingdom's Eastern Conquest Army, was uttered. 四人は慌てて担当者の元に急ぐ。 The remaining one looked around nervously. Trembling unsteadily amidst the four magic staffs pointed at him, he stepped forward.

メイティールの隣に控えている秘書官が黙ってそれを受け取る。 Mateil shifts her eyes to the next person in line, as if the man had already faded from her consciousness.

「やっと雑用が片付いたわ。研究はどうなっているかしら」 Mateil said in a laboratory made by remodeling the grandest dance hall in Kurtheite Castle. Inside the room, fifteen mages in black robes were divided into three groups working.

老若二人のローブの男女が皇女の前に進み出た。

「破城槌の改良図の結果が出ました」 The old man approaches her. メイティールは手を伸ばして、結果を受け取る。 Complex patterns were drawn on the paper.

「喰城虫の魔導陣も最適化が進んできたわね。これなら、王都の城門も抜けるわ。でも待って……、回路のこの部分が何かしっくり来ないわね。ここで魔力の流れが澱んでいるわ」

「すぐに検証いたしましょう」 Mateil ran her pen over one of the formulas comprising the magic circle. 老人はメイティールの修正した魔導陣を受け取る。

「もう一つの方はどうなっているのかしら」 Mateil spun the metal ring in her palm. It's a carriage part that came from the west, a component of astonishing precision and mechanism. It was the only thing that piqued the interest of Mateil, who was disappointed by the Kingdom's extremely low level of magic.

「申し訳ありません。現状ではこれが限界です」 A young woman in a robe presented a circular ring to Mateil. It is one size larger than the one in Mateil's hand. メイティールはその大小二つの金属の輪を回転させる。

「王国の方がなめらかに動くわね」 Mateil said with a face that genuinely couldn't understand, rather than blaming her subordinate.

「はい、六つの球を全て厳密に同じ大きさにすることが難しく」 The subordinate mage, perhaps knowing her boss's personality, spoke only what was necessary.

「貴方が試作した方が大きいのに誤差が大きい…………。こちらの軸受けの精度は貴方よりも一桁高いわね」

「おっしゃる通りでございます。金属の加工精度としては異常でございます。ご指示通り、馬車の他の部品を調べましたが、同様の精度は全く見られませんでした。また、この部品を使った馬車は十台に一台もなく、全て西方から来ていた物です」 The magic level of the Kingdom she completely looked down upon, and the small ring that suddenly caught her eye within it. Even to her, it was anomalous. She couldn't help but think it popped out of nowhere.

「西方…………、ベルトルドね。早急に調べなければいけないわ。ダゴバードはまだベルトルドを落としていないのかしら」 Mateil bit her nail. 知りたいことをすぐに知れない時の彼女の癖だ。

「未だ連絡はありません」

「メイティール殿下」

「東部攻略軍司令官」

「何をやっているのよあの男は。しかも馬竜の半分以上を失ったって。どうしたらそれだけ完璧に負けられるの」 Without caring about the eyes around her, Mateil shouted. The military force nurtured over ten years was lost. It means her actions, having won a sweeping victory in the east, will also be greatly restricted.

「……紫色の煙で馬竜の呼吸が止められたって……毒を使われたということ。紫……どういうこと、アレは馬竜には効かないはずでしょ。でも、同じ症状だわ。私たちに渡したのは劣化品という可能性は考慮したわ。それに、ガス化して…………即効性。呼吸器官に関係するのね」 Despite the envoy being in front of her, Mateil withdrew into her own mind.

「あの竜の解剖結果を持ってきなさい。……本国からリーザベルトを呼ぶ必要があるわね」 She issued instructions to her subordinates. In her brightly shining eyes, anger and curiosity were swirling.

Accompanied halfway by Craig's Knight Order heading for Kazel, we returned to the royal capital. カゼルにはまだ五千を超える帝国軍が立てこもっている。 There should still be nearly forty horse-dragons left. しかも、クルトハイト陥落により王国本軍は東部を重視せざるを得ない。

I have to try it out first. Excusing myself to Fulsi, I put the solid and some of the experimental equipment into a pot, soaked them in water, and placed it on the stove.

「王国東部の状況は切迫しているのです」 The first to open his mouth was Leonardo. グリニシアス公爵領はクルトハイトの隣だ。 According to what Leonardo said indignantly, the reason Kurtheite fell so easily was because the eastern lords' forces had become idle troops.

While we were holding an information session on the honey business in Berthold, Grand Duke Zangrich of Kurtheite, who had been reluctant to dispatch troops, was apparently slowly heading toward the royal capital following the withdrawal of the First Knight Order.

The Prime Minister's assessment was that Zangrich probably tried to push the direct confrontation with the horse-dragons onto Craig and Eufilia.

As a result, the eastern lords' forces became idle troops useless in both the eastern and western battles. With the center of the east suddenly falling, half of the eastern lords reportedly rushed back to their own territories in a panic.

Currently, Zangrich has been stripped of his command over the eastern lords' forces and confined to his home in the royal capital. The remaining eastern lords' forces are reportedly preparing for the Empire in the Grinicias Duke's territory under the Commander of the First Knight Order.

The reason for this is the unexpectedly strong power of the Imperial Army holed up in Kurtheite.

What is imagined from the story is a standard magic often seen in games from my previous life: a fireball. I haven't heard that such magic-like magic exists.

It has a longer range than arrows shot down from the top of the city walls, and apparently even homes in to some extent by the caster's will. それが百数十人から放たれた。 Of course, the army was said to be many times larger, but that attack alone practically brought down the largest city in the east.

The reason they broke through the wyvern territory overhanging the great river was probably because they could deal with flying opponents using projectiles.

It's like a medieval city being attacked by an army armed with guns. There is absolutely no way to defend against it. And it goes without saying that conquering a fortified city armed with such projectiles is impossible. The First Knight Commander's army, aiming to recapture Kurtheite, apparently returned to Grinicias barely escaping with their lives.

「…………一言で言えば距離じゃな。魔術は距離が離れれば離れるほど魔力必要量が雪だるま式に増える。それなのに、帝国は魔術そのものを飛び道具として用いた。これは本来ならあり得ぬことじゃ」

「その魔力を感知する魔術はアンテナで発動しておる」 I see, the required amount increasing geometrically with distance and capacity is likely a common property when converting magical power into effects.

「帝国の魔導は、こちらの魔術に比べて魔力を効果に変換する効率が遙かに高いって考えた方が良さそうですね」 Probably, during the increasingly severe battles against monsters up until ten years ago, some sort of breakthrough occurred.

Simply flowing magical power into a weapon made of magic gold isn't something everyone can do. Probably, there is some kind of compatibility between the circuit of the magic tool and the circuit possessed by the person. What comes to mind is the unnatural way the horse-dragon unit held their reins.

「帝国の馬竜部隊なんですけど、全員が手綱を腕にぐるぐると巻き付けていたんですよ」 Normally, it's a dangerous act. In fact, not a few Imperial knights who fell off their horses while getting tangled with the horse-dragons suffered broken arms. The effects that home appliances exhibit using electricity are diverse, but the power source is standardized through outlets. Any home appliance will work if plugged into an outlet.

Alright, I'm starting to understand a little.

「つまり、魔力を効率的に魔導に変換することと、共通の魔道具を使用出来る仕組み。この二つが帝国の優位性と言うことですね」

He's the top mage in the Kingdom, and recently he's been called a genius unprecedented in the Kingdom's history, right? 「俺は魔力は全くなくて、魔術は使えないんですけど」

「赤い森に行ったのは帝国の魔導への対抗策のためだったのじゃろ。すでにこれだけの準備を整えておる。そろそろ何をやるか教えてくれても良かろう」 Fulsi grinned as he looked at the glassware lined up on the laboratory desk. It's typical of him to prioritize his own curiosity over the Kingdom's crisis.

Well, in this case, the opponent is a resource-rich country, though.

「お主はどうする」

「赤い森から取ってきた土の解析をします」 I looked at the experimental equipment on the table. More than a dozen kinds of glass and metal instruments. In my original world, they'd be petri dishes, test tubes, and the like. It has a much more authentic lineup than I expected, but to achieve what I want to do, trial and error will probably be necessary.

「魔導の術式、魔導陣の解析は分るわ。だけど、土がなんの役に立つの？」 Noel tilted her head.

「魔力に反応する物質を見つけるっていうのが近いかな。ほら、この部屋を覆っている黒い色素みたいな物を探すって言えばわかりやすいか？」

「そのごく僅かを見つけ出す方法があるんですよ」 In the terminology of my previous life, it's the screening of useful substances. It was a method mainly used to find antibiotics and anticancer drugs, though. In this case, it would be akin to searching for a new catalyst.

Let's assume Earth was transported to another world with magical power. The two species that would adapt to magical power first are humans and bacteria.

The reason is simple and shared: learning speed.

First of all, bacteria have a massive population and an overwhelmingly fast generational turnover. In the learning device common to life called DNA, these two elements are crucial. Offspring of different types are produced randomly, and individuals with genes that adapt even slightly to magical power gain an advantage in the competition for energy. Naturally, they leave many descendants, and from those descendants, offspring even more suited to magical power are more likely to be born. After that, it's just repetition.
